About Late Rules

Rules are applied automatically based on the employee's shift start time. Fixed deductions are subtracted from the gross daily wage, while percentage deductions are calculated based on the total monthly base salary.

Pro-Tip: Grace periods do not apply if an employee arrives after the specified threshold.
